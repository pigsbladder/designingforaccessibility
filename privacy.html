Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

What data we collect and how we use it (short answer: very little).

Last updated: March 2026

Overview

Designing for Accessibility is committed to protecting your privacy. We believe that privacy is fundamental to a trustworthy internet. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

What We Collect

Information You Provide

We only collect information when you voluntarily provide it to us. If you contact us by email, we collect your email address and the content of your message. This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry and will not be shared, sold, or used for marketing purposes.

Information We Don't Collect

  • No tracking cookies. We do not use tracking cookies or similar technologies to monitor your browsing behaviour.
  • No personal data collection. We do not collect personal information like names, phone numbers, or location data unless you explicitly provide it.
  • No account creation required. You can browse and read all our content without creating an account or providing any personal information.
  • No analytics that track users. We may use privacy-friendly analytics to understand which pages are popular, but these tools do not identify individual users or track you across websites.

Third-Party Services

Designing for Accessibility is built with a focus on minimal external dependencies. We do not use Google Fonts or other services that might track your behaviour. Our site uses system fonts whenever possible.

If we do use third-party services in the future, we will only use services that respect your privacy and will update this policy accordingly.

Email Communication

If you contact us by email, we may occasionally respond or follow up. Your email address will be kept private and used only for communication purposes. We will not add you to a mailing list or newsletter without your explicit consent. You can always ask to be removed from communications at any time.

External Links

Our site contains links to external websites, including social media (LinkedIn, BlueSky) and Ko-fi. These external sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any information.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect any information you provide. However, no system is completely secure. If you have concerns about security, please reach out to us at hello@designingforaccessibility.com.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Know what information we have collected about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt out of any communications

To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@designingforaccessibility.com.

Policy Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Significant changes will be noted on this page with an updated "Last modified" date. Your continued use of the site following such changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about our privacy practices or this policy, please reach out:

  • Email: hello@designingforaccessibility.com
  • BlueSky: @dfaccessibility.bsky.social
  • LinkedIn: Designing for Accessibility
