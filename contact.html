Professional Services

Work With Me

I take on a limited number of consulting projects each year. If your team is working on accessibility and needs expert guidance, get in touch and let's talk scope.

The guides on this site are free. My time is not. These are paid engagements for organisations that need hands-on accessibility expertise, not just reading material.

Accessibility Audit

A structured WCAG 2.1 or 2.2 Level AA audit of your product, interface, or codebase. You get a clear, prioritised report you can actually act on.

Typically includes
  • Manual and automated testing against WCAG 2.1/2.2 AA
  • Prioritised findings with severity ratings
  • Remediation guidance per issue
  • Written report delivered in your preferred format

VPAT & ACR Writing

Expert VPAT preparation for federal procurement and enterprise sales. I write or review your Accessibility Conformance Report so it accurately represents your product and holds up to scrutiny.

Typically includes
  • Full VPAT completion (WCAG 2.1 or 2.2 edition)
  • Section 508 and EN 301 549 mapping where required
  • Review and correction of existing VPATs
  • Guidance on supporting known exceptions

Design & UX Review

Accessibility-focused review of your Figma designs, prototype, or design system before anything goes to build. Catching issues at the design stage costs a fraction of fixing them in code.

Typically includes
  • Color contrast, focus order, and heading structure review
  • Interactive component and state review
  • Design system token and pattern recommendations
  • Annotated feedback delivered directly in your Figma file
